Digital Electronics.
Flip-flop is a bistable circuit that stores one bit of information.
Multiplexer is a combinational circuit that selects one of many inputs and forwards it to a single output.
Karnaugh map is a graphical method for simplifying Boolean expressions.
Counter is a sequential circuit that goes through a prescribed sequence of states on clock pulses.
Decoder is a combinational circuit that converts binary input codes into distinct output lines.
Logic gate is an elementary building block that performs a Boolean operation on one or more inputs.
Shift register is a group of flip-flops that moves stored bits one position on every clock pulse.
