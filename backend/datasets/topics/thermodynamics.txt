Thermodynamics.
Entropy is a measure of the disorder or the unavailable energy of a system.
Enthalpy is the total heat content of a system at constant pressure.
Carnot cycle is an ideal reversible cycle that gives the maximum possible efficiency between two temperatures.
Heat engine is a device that converts heat energy into useful mechanical work.
Ideal gas is a hypothetical gas whose molecules have no volume and no mutual attraction.
Isothermal process is a process that takes place at constant temperature.
First law of thermodynamics is the statement that energy can be transformed but neither created nor destroyed.
