Chapter 5: Carnot Cycle

Learning objectives
- Define Carnot cycle and explain where it is used.
- Define first law of thermodynamics and explain where it is used.

Carnot Cycle
Carnot cycle is an ideal reversible cycle that gives the maximum possible efficiency between
two temperatures. In practice, Carnot cycle is combined with isothermal process to solve
larger problems. The main advantage of Carnot cycle is that it is simple to understand and
efficient in practice. For example, consider a small case in Thermodynamics where Carnot
cycle is applied step by step.

First Law Of Thermodynamics:
First law of thermodynamics is the statement that energy can be transformed but neither
created nor destroyed. For example, consider a small case in Thermodynamics where first law
of thermodynamics is applied step by step. In practice, first law of thermodynamics is
combined with Carnot cycle to solve larger problems. Note that first law of thermodynamics
depends on the assumptions made about the problem.

Isothermal Process
Isothermal process is a process that takes place at constant temperature. The main advantage
of isothermal process is that it is simple to understand and efficient in practice.

Key points
- Remember the definition of Carnot cycle.
- First law of thermodynamics is related to isothermal process.

Summary
In this unit we studied Carnot cycle, first law of thermodynamics and isothermal process.
