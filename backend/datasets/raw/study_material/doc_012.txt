Counter - Study Notes

Learning objectives
- Define counter and explain where it is used.
- Define multiplexer and explain where it is used.

Counter
Counter is a sequential circuit that goes through a prescribed sequence of states on clock pulses. Students should remember that counter is closely related to decoder. The main advantage of counter is that it is simple to understand and efficient in practice.

Multiplexer
Multiplexer is a combinational circuit that selects one of many inputs and forwards it to a single output. For example, consider a small case in Digital Electronics where multiplexer is applied step by step.

Shift Register:
Shift register is a group of flip-flops that moves stored bits one position on every clock pulse. A common mistake is to confuse shift register with counter. The main advantage of shift register is that it is simple to understand and efficient in practice. This idea is widely used in engineering design.

Decoder:
Decoder is a combinational circuit that converts binary input codes into distinct output lines. A common mistake is to confuse decoder with Karnaugh map.

Flip-Flop
Flip-flop is a bistable circuit that stores one bit of information. A common mistake is to confuse flip-flop with logic gate.

Summary
In this unit we studied counter, multiplexer, shift register, decoder and flip-flop. These ideas form the foundation for further topics in Digital Electronics.
