Multiplexer - Study Notes

Learning objectives
- Define multiplexer and explain where it is used.
- Define flip-flop and explain where it is used.

Multiplexer
Multiplexer is a combinational circuit that selects one of many
inputs and forwards it to a single output. In practice,
multiplexer is combined with decoder to solve larger problems.
Students should remember that multiplexer is closely related to
decoder. This idea is widely used in academic research.

Flip-Flop:
Flip-flop is a bistable circuit that stores one bit of
information. The main advantage of flip-flop is that it is
simple to understand and efficient in practice. This idea is
widely used in real world systems. For example, consider a small
case in Digital Electronics where flip-flop is applied step by
step.

Shift Register:
Shift register is a group of flip-flops that moves stored bits
one position on every clock pulse. Students should remember that
shift register is closely related to flip-flop. In practice,
shift register is combined with flip-flop to solve larger
problems.

Logic Gate
Logic gate is an elementary building block that performs a
Boolean operation on one or more inputs. In practice, logic gate
is combined with decoder to solve larger problems.

Counter
Counter is a sequential circuit that goes through a prescribed
sequence of states on clock pulses. A common mistake is to
confuse counter with logic gate. For example, consider a small
case in Digital Electronics where counter is applied step by
step.

Summary
In this unit we studied multiplexer, flip-flop, shift register,
logic gate and counter. These ideas form the foundation for
further topics in Digital Electronics.
