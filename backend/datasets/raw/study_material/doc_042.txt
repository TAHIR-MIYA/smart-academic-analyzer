UNIT 4: NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

Cosine Similarity
Cosine similarity is a measure of the angle between two vectors used to compare documents. A common mistake is to confuse cosine similarity with TF-IDF. Students should remember that cosine similarity is closely related to TF-IDF.

Tokenization
Tokenization is the splitting of text into smaller units such as words or sentences. For example, consider a small case in Natural Language Processing where tokenization is applied step by step.

Tf-Idf
Tf-idf is a weighting scheme that scores a term by its frequency in a document and its rarity across documents. This idea is widely used in academic research. In practice, TF-IDF is combined with language model to solve larger problems.

Named Entity Recognition
Named entity recognition is the task of locating and classifying names of people, places and organisations in text. For example, consider a small case in Natural Language Processing where named entity recognition is applied step by step. This idea is widely used in engineering design. A common mistake is to confuse named entity recognition with tokenization.

Key points
- Remember the definition of cosine similarity.
- Tokenization is related to named entity recognition.

Summary
In this unit we studied cosine similarity, tokenization, TF-IDF and named entity recognition. These ideas form the foundation for further topics in Natural Language Processing.
