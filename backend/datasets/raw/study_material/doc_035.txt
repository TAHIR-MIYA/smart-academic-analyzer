Karnaugh Map - Study Notes

Learning objectives
- Define Karnaugh map and explain where it is used.
- Define multiplexer and explain where it is used.

Karnaugh Map
Karnaugh map is a graphical method for simplifying Boolean
expressions. Students should remember that Karnaugh map is
closely related to multiplexer.

Multiplexer
Multiplexer is a combinational circuit that selects one of
many inputs and forwards it to a single output. A common
mistake is to confuse multiplexer with flip-flop. Students
should remember that multiplexer is closely related to flip-
flop.

Flip-Flop
Flip-flop is a bistable circuit that stores one bit of
information. This idea is widely used in everyday software. A
common mistake is to confuse flip-flop with shift register.
Students should remember that flip-flop is closely related to
shift register.

Summary
In this unit we studied Karnaugh map, multiplexer and flip-
flop. These ideas form the foundation for further topics in
Digital Electronics.
