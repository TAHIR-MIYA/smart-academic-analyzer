Chapter 6: Heat Engine

Heat Engine:
Heat engine is a device that converts heat energy into useful mechanical work. The main advantage of heat engine is that it is simple to understand and efficient in practice.

Enthalpy
Enthalpy is the total heat content of a system at constant pressure. For example, consider a small case in Thermodynamics where enthalpy is applied step by step.

Carnot Cycle
Carnot cycle is an ideal reversible cycle that gives the maximum possible efficiency between two temperatures. A common mistake is to confuse Carnot cycle with first law of thermodynamics.

Entropy
Entropy is a measure of the disorder or the unavailable energy of a system. The main advantage of entropy is that it is simple to understand and efficient in practice. For example, consider a small case in Thermodynamics where entropy is applied step by step. Students should remember that entropy is closely related to enthalpy.

Isothermal Process
Isothermal process is a process that takes place at constant temperature. For example, consider a small case in Thermodynamics where isothermal process is applied step by step. A common mistake is to confuse isothermal process with entropy. In practice, isothermal process is combined with entropy to solve larger problems.

Summary
In this unit we studied heat engine, enthalpy, Carnot cycle, entropy and isothermal process. These ideas form the foundation for further topics in Thermodynamics.
