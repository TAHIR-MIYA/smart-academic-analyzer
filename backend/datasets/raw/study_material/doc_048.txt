Stemming - Study Notes

Learning objectives
- Define stemming and explain where it is used.
- Define named entity recognition and explain where it is used.

Stemming
Stemming is a rule based reduction of words to a common root form by removing suffixes. This idea is widely used in engineering design.

Named Entity Recognition:
Named entity recognition is the task of locating and classifying names of people, places and organisations in text. This idea is widely used in everyday software. Students should remember that named entity recognition is closely related to stemming. A common mistake is to confuse named entity recognition with stemming.

Cosine Similarity
Cosine similarity is a measure of the angle between two vectors used to compare documents. Note that cosine similarity depends on the assumptions made about the problem.

Key points
- Remember the definition of stemming.
- Named entity recognition is related to cosine similarity.

Summary
In this unit we studied stemming, named entity recognition and cosine similarity. These ideas form the foundation for further topics in Natural Language Processing.
