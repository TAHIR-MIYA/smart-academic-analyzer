Chapter 1: Cosine Similarity

Cosine Similarity:
Cosine similarity is a measure of the angle between two vectors used to compare
documents. In practice, cosine similarity is combined with TF-IDF to solve larger
problems.

Named Entity Recognition
Named entity recognition is the task of locating and classifying names of people,
places and organisations in text. A common mistake is to confuse named entity
recognition with cosine similarity.

Part-Of-Speech Tagging
Part-of-speech tagging is the assignment of grammatical categories such as noun
or verb to each word. For example, consider a small case in Natural Language
Processing where part-of-speech tagging is applied step by step. Students should
remember that part-of-speech tagging is closely related to cosine similarity.

Stemming
Stemming is a rule based reduction of words to a common root form by removing
suffixes. Note that stemming depends on the assumptions made about the problem.
The main advantage of stemming is that it is simple to understand and efficient
in practice.

Language Model
Language model is a model that assigns probabilities to sequences of words. In
practice, language model is combined with named entity recognition to solve
larger problems. A common mistake is to confuse language model with named entity
recognition.

Summary
In this unit we studied cosine similarity, named entity recognition, part-of-
speech tagging, stemming and language model. These ideas form the foundation for
further topics in Natural Language Processing.
