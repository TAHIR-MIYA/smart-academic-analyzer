Chapter 6: Flip-Flop

Flip-Flop:
Flip-flop is a bistable circuit that stores one bit of information. For example, consider a small case in Digital Electronics where flip-flop is applied step by step. A common mistake is to confuse flip-flop with multiplexer.

Shift Register
Shift register is a group of flip-flops that moves stored bits one position on every clock pulse. This idea is widely used in industry.

Counter:
Counter is a sequential circuit that goes through a prescribed sequence of states on clock pulses. Note that counter depends on the assumptions made about the problem. For example, consider a small case in Digital Electronics where counter is applied step by step.

Decoder:
Decoder is a combinational circuit that converts binary input codes into distinct output lines. The main advantage of decoder is that it is simple to understand and efficient in practice. For example, consider a small case in Digital Electronics where decoder is applied step by step. In practice, decoder is combined with shift register to solve larger problems.

Summary
In this unit we studied flip-flop, shift register, counter and decoder. These ideas form the foundation for further topics in Digital Electronics.
