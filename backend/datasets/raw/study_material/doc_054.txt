Lecture Notes - Thermodynamics

Learning objectives
- Define first law of thermodynamics and explain where it is used.
- Define isothermal process and explain where it is used.

First Law Of Thermodynamics
First law of thermodynamics is the statement that energy can be transformed but neither
created nor destroyed. For example, consider a small case in Thermodynamics where first law
of thermodynamics is applied step by step. Students should remember that first law of
thermodynamics is closely related to entropy. This idea is widely used in academic
research.

Isothermal Process:
Isothermal process is a process that takes place at constant temperature. Note that
isothermal process depends on the assumptions made about the problem.

Enthalpy
Enthalpy is the total heat content of a system at constant pressure. Note that enthalpy
depends on the assumptions made about the problem. For example, consider a small case in
Thermodynamics where enthalpy is applied step by step. A common mistake is to confuse
enthalpy with heat engine.

Entropy
Entropy is a measure of the disorder or the unavailable energy of a system. In practice,
entropy is combined with enthalpy to solve larger problems. Students should remember that
entropy is closely related to enthalpy. Note that entropy depends on the assumptions made
about the problem.

Key points
- Remember the definition of first law of thermodynamics.
- Isothermal process is related to entropy.

Summary
In this unit we studied first law of thermodynamics, isothermal process, enthalpy and
entropy. These ideas form the foundation for further topics in Thermodynamics.
