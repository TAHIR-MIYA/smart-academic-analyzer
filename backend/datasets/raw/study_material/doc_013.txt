Chapter 7: Firewall

Firewall:
Firewall is a security device that filters incoming and outgoing traffic according to rules. In practice, firewall is combined with DNS to solve larger problems. This idea is widely used in engineering design.

Ip Addressing
Ip addressing is the scheme used to identify every host and network on the internet. This idea is widely used in real world systems.

Dns
Dns is a distributed system that translates domain names into IP addresses. For example, consider a small case in Computer Networks where DNS is applied step by step. A common mistake is to confuse DNS with routing.

Subnetting
Subnetting is the division of a large network into smaller logical networks. The main advantage of subnetting is that it is simple to understand and efficient in practice.

Summary
In this unit we studied firewall, IP addressing, DNS and subnetting. These ideas form the foundation for further topics in Computer Networks.
