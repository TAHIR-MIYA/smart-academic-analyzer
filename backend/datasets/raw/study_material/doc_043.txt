Unit 6: Digital Electronics

Karnaugh Map
Karnaugh map is a graphical method for simplifying Boolean expressions. In practice, Karnaugh map is combined with shift register to solve larger problems.

Counter:
Counter is a sequential circuit that goes through a prescribed sequence of states on clock pulses. In practice, counter is combined with flip-flop to solve larger problems.

Multiplexer
Multiplexer is a combinational circuit that selects one of many inputs and forwards it to a single output. A common mistake is to confuse multiplexer with Karnaugh map.

Decoder
Decoder is a combinational circuit that converts binary input codes into distinct output lines. A common mistake is to confuse decoder with flip-flop. Students should remember that decoder is closely related to flip-flop.

Key points
- Remember the definition of Karnaugh map.
- Counter is related to decoder.

Summary
In this unit we studied Karnaugh map, counter, multiplexer and decoder. These ideas form the foundation for further topics in Digital Electronics.
