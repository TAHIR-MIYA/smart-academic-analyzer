Learning objectives
- Define language model and explain where it is used.
- Define named entity recognition and explain where it is used.

Language Model
Language model is a model that assigns probabilities to sequences of words. A common
mistake is to confuse language model with part-of-speech tagging.

Named Entity Recognition:
Named entity recognition is the task of locating and classifying names of people,
places and organisations in text. Students should remember that named entity
recognition is closely related to cosine similarity.

Cosine Similarity:
Cosine similarity is a measure of the angle between two vectors used to compare
documents. For example, consider a small case in Natural Language Processing where
cosine similarity is applied step by step.

Summary
In this unit we studied language model, named entity recognition and cosine
similarity. These ideas form the foundation for further topics in Natural Language
Processing.
