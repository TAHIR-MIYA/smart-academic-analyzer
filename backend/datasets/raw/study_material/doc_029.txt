Language Model
Language model is a model that assigns probabilities to sequences of
words. This idea is widely used in industry. Note that language model
depends on the assumptions made about the problem. A common mistake is
to confuse language model with TF-IDF.

Stemming
Stemming is a rule based reduction of words to a common root form by
removing suffixes. In practice, stemming is combined with tokenization
to solve larger problems. For example, consider a small case in
Natural Language Processing where stemming is applied step by step.

Tokenization
Tokenization is the splitting of text into smaller units such as words
or sentences. In practice, tokenization is combined with part-of-
speech tagging to solve larger problems. For example, consider a small
case in Natural Language Processing where tokenization is applied step
by step. This idea is widely used in everyday software.

Key points
- Remember the definition of language model.
- Stemming is related to tokenization.
